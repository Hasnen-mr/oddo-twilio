FAQ

Is the module free?

Yes. The module is free to install. An initial monthly fair-usage allowance covers normal business use. Higher volume is available through In-App Purchase add-ons. Twilio minutes remain billed by Twilio.

Are Twilio minutes included?

No. Calls are billed by Twilio on your account. You need an upgraded Twilio account with balance for production use.

Is my data secure?

Credentials live in your Odoo configuration, calls use your Twilio account, and contacts stay in your Odoo database.
